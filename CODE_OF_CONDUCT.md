# Code of conduct

Tatanka Charts is built by and for traders. Everyone taking part here (issues, pull requests, discussions) is
expected to keep it a place where people can ask questions and get help without being put down.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including toward people new to trading or to code.
- Keep it on the subject: the app, its data feeds, its indicators and its docs.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not of the person.
- Respect people's privacy: no one's account names, positions, P&L, keys or personal details posted without
  their consent.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Spam, advertising, or promoting signal services, courses or paid groups.
- Trading advice presented as a sure thing, or pressure to trade.
- Publishing someone else's private information.

## What happens

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and
may block anyone from the project, temporarily or for good, for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report something privately, email tatankatradingllc@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in the project's spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
